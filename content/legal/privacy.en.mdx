## What we collect

- **Account data** — when you sign in (emailed code, Google or GitHub) we receive your email address, plus the name and profile picture a Google or GitHub account provides. We store these plus your language preference.
- **Usage data** — what the Service needs to run for you, such as your sessions and the profile photo you upload.
- **Technical data** — standard server logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) kept briefly for security and debugging.

## Why we use it

To provide the Service, send transactional email (such as sign-in codes), keep the Service secure, and comply with law. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Cookies

We use only cookies the Service needs to work: a session cookie to keep you signed in, and small preference cookies for language and theme. No advertising cookies.

## Where it lives and who sees it

Data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. It is shared only with the providers required to run the Service — Google and GitHub (sign-in) and our email provider (transactional email) — each under their own privacy terms.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account from Settings permanently removes your profile and sessions from our systems.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase your data, or to object to processing. You can do most of this yourself in Settings; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Changes and contact

We will announce material changes to this policy before they take effect. Questions: {appConfig.supportEmail}.
